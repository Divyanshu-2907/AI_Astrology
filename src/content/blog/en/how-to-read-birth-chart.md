## What is a Birth Chart (Kundali)?

A birth chart, also known as a *Kundali* or *Janam Patrika* in Vedic astrology, is a snapshot of the sky at the exact moment you were born. It shows the precise mathematical positions of the planets and constellations from the perspective of your birthplace.

While it may look like a complex mathematical diagram full of numbers and symbols, reading a North Indian style Kundali is actually quite logical once you understand the basic framework.

Here is a step-by-step beginner's guide to decoding your own birth chart!

---

## Step 1: Understand the Diamond Shape (The North Indian Chart)

In Vedic astrology, the most common chart format used in Northern India is a square divided into 12 smaller sections (mostly triangles and diamonds). 

### The Fixed Houses
The most important rule to remember for a North Indian chart is that **the Houses are fixed, but the Zodiac Signs rotate.**

- The large diamond at the top-center of the chart is **always the 1st House**.
- From there, you count the houses counter-clockwise.
- So, the triangle to the top-left of the center diamond is the 2nd House, the one below it is the 3rd House, the diamond on the left is the 4th House, and so on, until you complete the circle back to the 12th House.

## Step 2: Look at the Numbers (The Zodiac Signs)

You will see numbers written inside each of the 12 boxes. **These numbers do NOT represent the house.** Remember, the houses are fixed in place.

Instead, **these numbers represent the Zodiac Signs** (Rasis).

Each of the 12 zodiac signs has a designated number:
1.  **Aries** (Mesha)
2.  **Taurus** (Vrishabha)
3.  **Gemini** (Mithuna)
4.  **Cancer** (Karka)
5.  **Leo** (Simha)
6.  **Virgo** (Kanya)
7.  **Libra** (Tula)
8.  **Scorpio** (Vrishchika)
9.  **Sagittarius** (Dhanu)
10. **Capricorn** (Makara)
11. **Aquarius** (Kumbha)
12. **Pisces** (Meena)

### Finding Your Ascendant (Rising Sign)
Look at the top-center diamond (the 1st House). What number is written inside it? 

If the number is 5, it means your 1st House contains Leo (the 5th sign). Therefore, your **Ascendant or Rising Sign is Leo**. 

Since the signs follow sequentially, if the 1st House is 5 (Leo), the 2nd House will contain 6 (Virgo), the 3rd House will contain 7 (Libra), and so forth.

## Step 3: Find the Planets (Grahas)

Next, you will see abbreviations or symbols inside the houses. These represent the planets that were physically located in that segment of the sky when you were born.

Common abbreviations used in Kundalis:
- **Su:** Sun (Surya)
- **Mo:** Moon (Chandra)
- **Ma:** Mars (Mangal)
- **Me:** Mercury (Budh)
- **Ju:** Jupiter (Guru/Brihaspati)
- **Ve:** Venus (Shukra)
- **Sa:** Saturn (Shani)
- **Ra:** Rahu (North Node)
- **Ke:** Ketu (South Node)

### Putting it Together: House + Sign + Planet
To read a placement, you combine the House (the physical box), the Sign (the number in the box), and the Planet (the abbreviation).

**Example:**
Let's say in the left-side diamond (the 4th House), you see the number **8** and the abbreviation **Mo**.
- The 4th House represents the Mother and Home.
- The number 8 is Scorpio.
- "Mo" is the Moon.

**Interpretation:** Your Moon is in Scorpio in the 4th House. Because the Moon is debilitated (weakest) in Scorpio, this placement might indicate emotional turbulence at home or a complex relationship with your mother.

## Step 4: The Ascendant Lord (Lagnesh)

The planet that rules the zodiac sign in your 1st House is called the *Ascendant Lord* or *Lagnesh*. This is the most important planet in your entire chart! It acts as the CEO of your life.

Find where this planet sits in your chart. The house it occupies shows where your primary life focus, physical energy, and life purpose will be directed.

- *Example:* If your Ascendant is Aries (Number 1), your Ascendant Lord is Mars. If Mars is sitting in your 10th House, your life purpose will be heavily focused on career, public status, and leadership.
